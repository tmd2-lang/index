FAQ

Good questions.
Straight answers.

The things pharmacies ask us most,
answered plainly.

Is Dosedrop a pharmacy?

Dosedrop is not a pharmacy. It's delivery software for pharmacies and hospital outpatient pharmacies; we don't dispense or sell medication.

How long does setup take?

Most pharmacies are up and running in a day. We train your team and drivers in person or over a video call.

How do you handle patient data?

Patient details are encrypted, and drivers only see what they need for the delivery in front of them.

Can I use my own drivers?

Yes. Your in-house drivers get the Dosedrop app, or you can mix them with on-demand couriers and our driver fleet.

What kind of drones do you use?

Long-range delivery drones, used for recurring routes to places that are hard to reach by road.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on how many deliveries you send each month, and the price per delivery drops as volume grows. Get in touch for a quote.

Can patients pay at the door?

Yes. Patients can pay by card, tap or cash when the order arrives, and each payment is matched to the right prescription.

Can you deliver controlled or refrigerated medication?

Yes. Controlled substances get an ID and signature check, fridge items travel in cold bags, and everything follows your local rules.

Do you offer virtual care or prescribing?

Not today. Dosedrop focuses on delivery and works alongside the virtual care tools you already use.
