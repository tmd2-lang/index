Frequently asked questions

What is Tally?

Tally is a notepad that does math. You type calculations as plain text, like dinner $84 / 3, and the answer appears next to each line as you type. It understands currencies, units, dates and time zones.

Does Tally run on Windows and Linux?

Yes. Tally runs on Mac, Windows 10 and 11, and 64-bit Linux. It's the same engine everywhere, so every line gives the same answer, and one license can mix all three.

Does Tally work offline?

Yes. Every calculation happens on your computer. Exchange rates need a connection to refresh; offline, Tally uses the last rates it saved.

Do I really pay only once?

Yes. No subscription. Buy it once and keep it, with updates included.

Is there a free trial?

Seven days, with everything unlocked. No account and no card needed.

Is there a student or teacher discount?

Half price. Write to us from your school email and we'll send you a code.

Where do exchange rates come from?

Public exchange-rate feeds, refreshed every hour while you're online. Each sheet shows when its rates were last updated.

How do I move my license to another computer?

Open Settings, choose License, and click Deactivate. Then enter the same key on the new computer.
