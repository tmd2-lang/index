Finding out what’s happening on campus should be the easy part.

A free dinner, a club meeting, a movie on the lawn. You should be able to see what’s happening, when it starts, and where to go. That’s what we’re building Ligo to do.

What happens
after I download?

  • Choose your school and your feed fills with what's happening there, tonight and all week.

  • Tap follow and their posts, events and news land right in your feed.

  • Search by event, place or category, or browse rows like Free Food.

  • Bookmark what looks good and send it to the group chat in a tap.

  • That's the whole point. Less missing out, more showing up.

A word to the club leaders

You don't just go to campus events. You make them.

You book the room, design the flyer and post it everywhere, and half of campus still hears about it the morning after. Ligo puts your event in front of the students looking for something to do tonight. No buried posts, no lost group chats.
